About Apex

Apex is a research-driven, specialty biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing innovative medicines that help people build families and live better lives.

Through research, development, manufacturing, and commercialization, Apex works to address important and often complex healthcare needs. The company combines scientific expertise with a strong commitment to patients, healthcare professionals, and the communities it serves.

Reproductive Medicine & Maternal Health

Apex has a longstanding commitment to reproductive medicine, providing treatments that support fertility care, including assisted reproductive technologies such as in vitro fertilization (IVF), as well as other areas of reproductive and maternal health.

Urology & Uro-Oncology

Apex develops and provides therapies addressing significant urological conditions, with a growing focus on uro-oncology and treatments for diseases such as bladder cancer.

Gastroenterology & the Microbiome

Apex is advancing treatments in gastroenterology and microbiome science, including innovative approaches to serious gastrointestinal conditions such as recurrent Clostridioides difficile (C. difficile) infection.

Driven by Science. Focused on Patients.

Across its therapeutic areas, Apex is committed to translating scientific research into meaningful treatments. By investing in innovation and addressing areas of significant unmet medical need, Apex aims to improve health outcomes and make a lasting difference in the lives of patients and families.
